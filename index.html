Mugilan Mariappan

I recently defended my Ph.D. in Computing Science at Simon Fraser University (October 2026), advised by Prof. Keval Vora. I design parallel systems for processing constantly changing data, with a focus on incremental computation over streaming graphs.

I built GraphBolt and DZiG, engines that update graph analytics incrementally as the graph changes, with BSP correctness guarantees. GraphBolt achieves a median 18× speedup over recomputation on billion-edge graphs on a single machine. I open-sourced the end-to-end parallel C++ system on GitHub, and also built Olfin, a concurrent graph ingestion system with tunable ordering guarantees. Before my Ph.D., I was a software engineer at athenaHealth for three years, and I interned at the LinkedIn Graph Systems Lab.

I am on the job market for research scientist, research engineer, and software engineering roles in systems.

I am part of the Parallel and Distributed Computing Lab (PDCL) and the Systems group at SFU. More details are in my CV (PDF).

Research Interests

Streaming graph processing, graph databases, and GNN infrastructure, with emphasis on parallelism, incremental computation, and dynamic data structures.
